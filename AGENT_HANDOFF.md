LitRPG progression fantasy. Launch target: 27 November 2026.

## Core Hooks (use in every asset)
1. A nineteen-year-old wedding DJ dies and keeps his memories.
2. Magic is sound and resonance, not sight.
3. A short hymn: “You are not lost. You are only sleeping. Wake now, and follow, and I will carry you home.”

## Key Motifs (visual and copy)
- Cyan system windows
- Resonance rings
- Faint hymn lines on dark stone
- Sound, waves, and resonance
- Dark-fantasy painterly style
- Avoid spoilers, faces when possible, and heavy “souls gathered” imagery

## Marketing Addendum Summary
### Positioning
Lead with the three hooks above in every ad, Reel, and blurb.

### Amazon Listing
- Title, subtitle, and first 300 characters must contain the three hooks.
- Categories: LitRPG, Progression Fantasy, Dark Fantasy.
- Keywords: LitRPG, progression fantasy, sound magic, hymn, reincarnation, memory.
- Launch price: $0.99 or free for 5 days, then $4.99.

### Ad Creative
- Parallel tests: static system window + hook, and 8–10 second resonance loop + hook.
- Concentrate spend on the winner after 14 days.

### BookTok / Reels
- One short loop per day for 30 days pre-launch.
- One hook per post.
- End with title and “27 Nov”.
- Consistent sound (two-note or feedback).

### Launch Sequence
- 14 days before: daily Reels + Amazon ad test.
- 7 days before: newsletter with free chapter.
- Launch day: price drop + ad spend increase + 5 Reel posts.
- Days 1–7: respond to every review and comment.
- Days 8–30: keep winning ad and Reel running.
